Wake response
Say a greeting instead of summon sound

When on, Akari randomly says “what’s up?”, “hey”, “hello”, “hi”, or “yeah?” with her TTS voice instead of playing Summon.mp3.

Continued conversation
Keep listening after replies (like Google Home)

When on, after Akari finishes speaking she listens for a follow-up without needing “hey Akari” again. Stays open for the window below, then goes idle if you say nothing.
